**What I've built**

**Reel Science** · [live](https://science-of-films.vercel.app)
An interactive guide to the science inside films. It has simulators for Interstellar's time dilation, tidal forces and rotating habitat, and chapters on the biology and physics of *Project Hail Mary*. Each film is data-driven, so adding one means adding data, not writing new pages.
`SvelteKit` `TypeScript` `Tailwind` `Vercel`

**Pyramid**
A personal practice tracker, built as the anti-todo-app. It records layered creative and learning practice over time with qualitative entries, a bullet-journal log and a slowly rotating activity sphere. It has no streaks, notifications or gamification. The notes are the asset, not the dots.
`SvelteKit` `Drizzle` `Turso` `better-auth` `Tailwind`

**Musynk**
Real-time listening rooms. The host controls playback and the queue, and everyone else stays in sync.
`React` `Vite` `Express` `Socket.IO` `Spotify OAuth`

**Now building: Tarot Journal**
A private, voice-first iOS journal for people learning to read tarot. You interpret the cards first, and an AI mentor reflects on *your* reading instead of giving you one of its own.
`Swift` `SwiftUI` `SwiftData` `Speech` `Claude API`
